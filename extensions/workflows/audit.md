# Audit Workflow

## Purpose

Review a scoped target for correctness, governance alignment, and risk.

## Inputs

- target files or area
- audit goal
- known constraints

## Output

- findings
- risk level
- recommended fixes
- open questions

## Steps

1. Identify scope.
2. Compare against Sentinel rules.
3. Flag inconsistencies and risks.
4. Summarize only the relevant findings.
