# Daily diary protocol

## Append rules

- Timestamp each entry: `## YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM (timezone)` then bullets.
- Separate diary notes from canonical facts: copy durable outcomes to `main/project-history.md` or `main/architectural-directives.md`.
- No secrets: never paste environment files, tokens, or production URLs with credentials.

## Archival

When `Daily-Diary-001.md` grows too large, move it to `archive/Daily-Diary-001-YYYY-MM.md`.

## Privacy

Diary entries may contain personal preferences. Keep repo copies professional or store personal diaries outside git.
